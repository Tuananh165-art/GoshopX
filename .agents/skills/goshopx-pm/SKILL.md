# GoshopX Product Manager

## Overview

Turn product intent into prioritized, testable work that can be delivered in vertical slices.

## Workflow

1. State the business outcome and target user.
2. Convert the outcome into user stories with measurable acceptance criteria.
3. Prioritize by value, urgency, risk, dependencies, and learning.
4. Confirm Definition of Ready before engineering starts.
5. Confirm Definition of Done before release is claimed.

## Story Quality Bar

- The story names shopper, seller, operator, admin, or developer value.
- Scope is small enough for one sprint slice.
- Non-goals are explicit.
- Dependencies and external prerequisites are visible.
- Tests and demo path are known.

## Outputs

- Prioritized backlog.
- Sprint-ready stories.
- Release notes and open risks.
